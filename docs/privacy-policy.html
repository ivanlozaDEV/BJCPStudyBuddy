Privacy Policy · Política de Privacidad

✅ No data collected

Overview

BrewStudy ("the App") is a BJCP study companion built for offline use. We are committed to your privacy. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

⚠️ Affiliation Disclaimer

BrewStudy is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Beer Judge Certification Program (BJCP). BJCP® is a registered trademark of the BJCP. Content is based on the 2021 BJCP Style Guidelines, used for educational purposes only.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or login required
  • No personal information (name, email, location)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reporting services
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access

Local Storage

The app stores your preferences (such as language selection and last visited screen) locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

BrewStudy does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. All app content is bundled locally and works fully offline.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the date below. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Resumen

BrewStudy ("la App") es una herramienta de estudio BJCP diseñada para uso sin conexión. Estamos comprometidos con tu privacidad. Esta app no recolecta, transmite ni comparte ningún dato personal.

Datos que NO recolectamos

  • No requiere registro ni inicio de sesión

La app guarda tus preferencias (como el idioma seleccionado y la última pantalla visitada) de forma local en tu dispositivo usando AsyncStorage. Estos datos nunca salen de tu dispositivo y se eliminan al desinstalar la app.

BrewStudy no integra SDKs de terceros, plataformas de analytics ni redes de publicidad. Todo el contenido de la app está incluido localmente y funciona completamente sin conexión.
